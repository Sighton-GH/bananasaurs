Tones and words

Six tones, one meaning each. Amber stays the one colour for “held or waiting”. Banana yellow lives only in the artwork.

Neutral0x64748B

Information. Nothing to do.

Accent0x2F5BD3

The thing to do here; links, focus.

Progress0x3B82F6

Underway, running.

Hold0xE0A526

Waiting, held, observe-only, needs a decision.

OK0x2E9A62

Done, healthy.

Danger0xD64545

Overdue, failed, destructive, moderation.
