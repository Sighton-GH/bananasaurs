# Bananasaurs brand direction

## Intent

A simple, friendly logo derived from the visual idea of a banana-shaped dinosaur plush. It must read as a mark at Discord-avatar size, not as a detailed mascot illustration.

## Current mark

`branding/logo.png` is the current mark: 1254 x 1254 PNG, 8-bit RGB, no alpha channel, 968 KB. It was generated with OpenAI Codex image tooling on 2026-08-18 and was previously sitting at the repository root under its original export filename. It is small enough to upload as the Discord application avatar unchanged, since Discord downscales server-side.

It does not yet satisfy the deliverables listed at the bottom of this file. Most immediately, it has no transparent background, so it will show a solid square behind the mark anywhere Discord renders a non-circular crop, and there is no one-colour variant. Treat it as the working mark, not the finished one.

## Primary concept: Banana-saur silhouette

- One curved banana body forming the dinosaur’s back and tail.
- Small, unmistakable dinosaur head at the stem end.
- Two or three minimal triangular back plates or a single tiny forelimb—never both if the mark becomes busy.
- One dot eye; no facial expression beyond gentle friendliness.
- Flat shapes, heavy clean outline, no texture, no plush fur, no gradients, no scene, no text inside the icon.
- Warm banana yellow fill, dark brown/charcoal outline, optional muted green accent used sparingly.
- Works in one color and remains identifiable at 32 px.

## Variants worth exploring

1. **Crescent profile:** the whole dinosaur is a banana crescent; clearest icon.
2. **Peel plates:** two small peel tips double as stegosaur plates; most distinctive if kept abstract.
3. **Negative-space head:** banana silhouette with a tiny dinosaur head cut into the inner curve; most logo-like and mature.

## Avoid

Plush rendering, 3D shading, large eyes, chibi proportions, detailed limbs, scenery, badges full of tiny text, Jurassic-style aggression, or copying the exact commercial plush design. The reference is a directional cue, not source artwork.

## Deliverables for a later visual pass

- Square icon with transparent background.
- One-color SVG/PNG.
- Dark- and light-background variants.
- Horizontal lockup with the word “Bananasaurs” in a rounded but restrained sans serif.
- Legibility sheet at 16, 32, 64, and 128 px.
